About MALI

Where tradition meets contemporary elegance

Welcome to MALI, where authentic Thai cuisine meets contemporary elegance. Located on the charming Nahalat Binyamin Street, we invite you to embark on a culinary journey through Thailand's vibrant flavors.

Our award-winning chefs craft each dish with precision, using the finest ingredients imported directly from Thailand. From fragrant curries to delicate stir-fries, every plate tells a story of tradition and passion.
